**Abyssal Fluidics**
*   **Core Idea:** Deep sea pressure and bioluminescent fluid dynamics in an obsidian void.
*   **Why it is not generic:** Uses viscosity and fluid displacement rather than rigid translations or basic digital glows.
*   **Motion language:** Heavy, viscous, delayed elastic settling with fluid displacement effects.
*   **Open:** Windows emerge like ink dropped in dense water, blooming outward until surface tension snaps them into a rigid rectangle.
*   **Close:** Implodes silently into a bright, temporary bioluminescent dot that rapidly dissipates in the current.
*   **Snap:** Crushing into the edge creates a viscous, brightly glowing ripple that stretches out until the new ratio (e.g., 1/3) is lit up along the bezel before settling.
*   **Focus:** The active window gently breathes with a faint deep-sea blue rim; unfocused windows sink slightly into darkness (z-depth blur).
*   **Seam:** A liquid channel where the colors of the two adjacent windows bleed and swirl slightly into each other.
*   **HUD:** Floats up from the bottom edge like rising air bubbles when summoned; clock is the largest bubble, tray/notifications/search are smaller clusters attached. Pops on exit.
*   **App-skin touch:** Firefox tabs look like sleek, dark, pressurized glass tubes.
*   **Felt reaction:** She appears to float, hair drifting; sometimes she looks toward bright bioluminescent flashes.
*   **Riskiest part:** Simulating the fluid/swirling shaders at the seam while maintaining strict 60+ fps during window movement.

**Magnetic Tape Brutalism**
*   **Core Idea:** Massive, heavy brutalist dark-grey slabs disrupted by severe VHS/magnetic tearing.
*   **Why it is not generic:** Dissonance between extreme physical weight and analog electrical instability.
*   **Motion language:** Heavy physical momentum interrupted by sudden, aggressive frame-tearing and scanline displacement.
*   **Open:** Slabs slide in with immense friction, sparking and tearing violently before slamming into alignment with a screen shake.
*   **Close:** The window suddenly loses horizontal sync, shearing into static bands that zap instantly into the side bezel.
*   **Snap:** Smashing a window to the edge causes a massive static burst; the static bands stretch exactly to the 1:1 or 1:2 boundary before solidifying into the new window size.
*   **Focus:** Inactive windows are desaturated and slightly covered in rolling scanlines; the active window is sharp, dark, and clear.
*   **Seam:** A literal crack of bright white noise static between the two windows, occasionally sparking.
*   **HUD:** Slamming Win key makes the screen aggressively roll vertically; the HUD (blocky, terminal-like text for clock/tray/search) is caught in the blanking interval between frames. Drops away instantly.
*   **App-skin touch:** Discord messages have a slight phosphor-bleed smearing to the right.
*   **Felt reaction:** She glitches out (model swaps with a wireframe or corrupted texture) exactly when the heavy impacts/snaps occur.
*   **Riskiest part:** Synchronizing global screen shake and aggressive tearing shaders with Wayland's strict frame timing.

**Obsidian Loom**
*   **Core Idea:** The desktop is a massive, dark mechanical loom weaving windows out of glowing glass threads.
*   **Why it is not generic:** Windows aren't boxes; they are tensioned fabrics of light woven dynamically in real time.
*   **Motion language:** High-tension snapping, taut strings, and rapid mechanical interlacing.
*   **Open:** Glowing threads shoot from the screen edges, rapidly crisscrossing and weaving the window surface into existence in milliseconds.
*   **Close:** The threads snap violently at the center, instantly unspooling back into the screen edges.
*   **Snap:** Dragging to the edge pulls the window threads taut; a glowing perpendicular thread shoots down at the 50% or 33% mark to indicate the new boundary before snapping into place.
*   **Focus:** Active window threads glow fiercely at the borders; inactive windows have their threads dimmed, looking like dark carbon fiber.
*   **Seam:** Tightly braided cables of light interlocking the two window edges; pulling a window stretches the braid until it snaps.
*   **HUD:** Drops from the top edge like a mechanical punch card; the clock, search, and tray icons are illuminated holes in a dark slate. Retracts mechanically.
*   **App-skin touch:** The terminal cursor isn't a block, but a glowing needle weaving the text line by line.
*   **Felt reaction:** Plucks the threads of closed or moving windows like a harp, emitting faint visual ripples.
*   **Riskiest part:** The geometry-heavy shader required to make window surfaces look authentically woven from threads rather than just a flat texture.

**Magnetic Anomaly**
*   **Core Idea:** Pitch black ferrofluid reacting to unseen magnetic fields to form window structures.
*   **Why it is not generic:** Replaces standard scaling and sliding with organic, spiky, magnetic surface tension and clustering.
*   **Motion language:** Spiky, magnetic attraction and repulsion, clustering into sharp geometric arrays.
*   **Open:** A puddle of black fluid erupts upward into sharp, metallic spikes that flatten instantly into a perfect, dark mirror window.
*   **Close:** The magnetic field cuts; the window instantly shatters into tiny liquid beads that get sucked into the nearest screen edge.
*   **Snap:** Approaching the edge creates intense magnetic spikes pulling the window; the spikes form a solid line at the 1:1 or 2:1 ratio, showing exactly where it will solidify.
*   **Focus:** Active window borders bristle with tiny, sharp, reactive ferro-spikes; inactive windows are dull, flat matte black.
*   **Seam:** A bristling zipper of interlocking fluid spikes that push against each other in constant tension.
*   **HUD:** Rises from the bottom corners like a smooth liquid droplet that splits into sharp, geometric shards containing the clock, tray, and search. Melts away.
*   **App-skin touch:** Discord servers/channels are held in separate floating, spiky magnetic clusters rather than a solid sidebar.
*   **Felt reaction:** Her clothing or hair behaves like ferrofluid, spiking toward the active window's magnetic field.
*   **Riskiest part:** Real-time rendering of believable metallic liquid/spiky displacement maps along the dynamic window borders.

**Sonar Relief**
*   **Core Idea:** The dark desktop is a flat plane that dynamically extrudes window shapes via echolocation pulses.
*   **Why it is not generic:** Everything is built from physical elevation changes in a unified matte dark surface, revealed only by subtle edge highlights.
*   **Motion language:** Smooth, heavy mechanical extrusions, ripples, and planar shifts in 3D space.
*   **Open:** A sonar ring pulses out; the center of the ring seamlessly extrudes upward from the desktop floor to form the window.
*   **Close:** The window depresses back into the floor perfectly, sending a dark, heavy shockwave outward.
*   **Snap:** Dragging to the edge causes the floor to preemptively fracture along the 1:3 or 1:2 fault lines; snapping drops the window exactly into that excavated socket.
*   **Focus:** Active window is physically elevated, casting a stark, long shadow; inactive windows sink slightly below the baseline level.
*   **Seam:** A deep, sheer cliff trench between the windows, where faint ambient occlusion shadows gather.
*   **HUD:** A section of the right edge folds outward like a mechanical flap, displaying glowing, debossed text for clock, search, and notifications. Folds flush on exit.
*   **App-skin touch:** Firefox UI elements (URL bar, tabs) are physically recessed into the dark window plane rather than drawn as boxes.
*   **Felt reaction:** She sits on the edge of the active window's elevated plane, dangling her feet into the trench.
*   **Riskiest part:** Creating a seamless, unified depth/shadow system across arbitrary Wayland surfaces so the extrusion illusion doesn't break at window corners.

**Emulsion**
*   **Core Idea:** Windows are projected onto dark, chemical-soaked photo paper, developing and burning into existence.
*   **Why it is not generic:** Uses chemical spread, burning, and exposure mechanics instead of physical movement or digital glitches.
*   **Motion language:** Rapid chemical blooming, exposure flashes, and caustic liquid creeping.
*   **Open:** A blindingly fast flash of light (exposure); the window darkens and develops into existence over 200ms from the center out.
*   **Close:** The window catches "fire" with a chemical burn, instantly scorching into black ash and dissolving.
*   **Snap:** Dragging to the edge spills developer fluid; the fluid creeps exactly along the grid lines of the 1:2 ratio, revealing the footprint before the window snaps in.
*   **Focus:** Active window is perfectly fixed and sharp; inactive windows slowly overexpose, becoming slightly darker, grainier, and lower-contrast.
*   **Seam:** A glowing, wet chemical gradient where the developer fluid pools between the two exposures.
*   **HUD:** Visible only under an invisible "safelight". Hovering an edge turns the screen corners a deep red, revealing the clock, search, and tray as developing silhouettes. Fades to black.
*   **App-skin touch:** Terminal text doesn't type out; it develops onto the screen line by line, dark grey on pitch black.
*   **Felt reaction:** She leaves temporary ghostly afterimages (like a long exposure photo) when she moves quickly.
*   **Riskiest part:** Implementing the chemical burn/dissolve shader on window close without stalling the KWin compositor.
