clockmost-desktop

Mario's daily-driver desktop: KDE Plasma with a custom shell dressed in the visual language of Arknights: Endfield — but built as real functional apps, not game screens. Windows, a dock, a top bar, a browser, a music player. The skin is Endfield; the content is a working desktop.

Every piece starts as a static 1920x1080 layout mock in components/, sharing one house style in ds-shell.css, so the whole desktop can be reviewed before anything is wired up. Design notes and work orders live in orders/.
