# Work order 05 - the dock (layout mock)
Read `orders/STYLE.md` first (all of it, especially "Desktop geometry"). Build ONE file: `components/ds-dock.html`.
Slug for shots: `05`. Background: `.stage` with one dimmed `.win.blur` window (~1300x760, centred slightly high,
title `FIREFOX`, empty body) behind, so the dock reads as an overlay.

The dock: floating plate, bottom centre, 72px tall, 12px above the screen bottom, width fits its content.
Material: `.frost` with 2px corners, plus its own small details (rule: own design, several layers): a hairline
along its top inner edge, a faint barcode + micro code `DOCK · 09 PINNED` at its far right end, tiny corner brackets.
Content, left to right:
- the app-drawer button: a 52px tile with a 2x2 square grid icon; separated by a hairline divider.
- 9 app tiles, 52px square, in the drawer's tile language (see `components/ds-app-drawer.html`: rounded 6px, dark
  glassy tile, inset shadow, coloured 3px underbar per app), each with its logo or letter: Firefox (use
  `firefox.svg`), Discord, Phonograph, Kitty, Dolphin, Steam, KeePassXC, Spectacle, Lutris (letters/simple glyphs).
- running state: a running app shows a small dot UNDER its tile (centred, 4px, `--ink2`); 2 windows = 2 dots.
  Firefox (2 windows), Discord, Phonograph, Kitty are running. The FOCUSED app (Firefox) gets a yellow underbar
  instead of its colour and a yellow dot.
- Discord has a notification: the orange `.notif` diamond at its tile's top-right corner.
- a hairline divider, then a trash-free "downloads" stack tile (tray-like icon) showing a tiny `3` badge.
Static hover example: above the Phonograph tile, a label tooltip (small dark plate, mono 11px `PHONOGRAPH`, a 6px
pointer) - as if the mouse is on it.
Second view `?state=hidden`: the dock is hidden; only a 4px-tall, 120px-wide faint handle at the bottom centre
hints it is there (the desktop is "clean").
