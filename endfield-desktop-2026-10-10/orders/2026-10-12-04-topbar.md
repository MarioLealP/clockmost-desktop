# Work order 04 - top bar + quick settings + clipboard picker (layout mock)
Read `orders/STYLE.md` first (all of it, especially "Desktop geometry"). Build ONE file: `components/ds-topbar.html`.
Slug for shots: `04`. Background: `.stage` with one dimmed floating window behind (a `.win.blur`, ~1200x700, centred,
title `PHONOGRAPH`, empty body) so the bar and panels read as an overlay on a desktop.

The top bar (40px, full width, `.frost`, hairline bottom) - shown in every state:
- Left: workspace chips `01 02 03` (small square-ish 26px chips, mono 11px; current one yellow `.sel`, others dim;
  `02` has a tiny dot meaning windows open), then a hairline divider, then the focused window's title in `.label`
  style (`PHONOGRAPH · GLASSY SKY`).
- Centre (exactly centred on the screen): the clock `20:41` (mono 15px, letter-spacing .18em, `--ink`) and after it
  the date in `.micro` (`MON 12 OCT`). Mario: "hit windows button and see a clock" - the clock is the hero.
- Right, in this order: a now-playing chip (tiny square art in lavender + `GLASSY SKY` in micro mono, max 160px),
  then tray icons as 28px buttons (line icons): clipboard, network (ethernet), volume, bluetooth, VPN (shield),
  notifications (bell with an orange `.notif` diamond on it), then a user chip `BENYLIN` with a small square avatar
  tile `BE`. 8px gaps. Icons `--ink2`; the icon whose panel is open is yellow.

States (`?state=`, default = `quick`):
- `bar`: only the bar.
- `quick`: the quick-settings panel open under the volume icon: `.panel` look (dark plate, brackets), 400px wide:
  - head: avatar tile + `benylin@clockmost` (body font) + `.micro` uptime line `UP 3D 04H · KERNEL 7.2.9-CACHYOS`.
  - a 2x3 grid of toggle tiles (each ~180x56: line icon, label, state word; ON tiles have a yellow left edge 3px and
    yellow icon): `ETHERNET · CONNECTED` (on), `BLUETOOTH · ROBIN` (on), `VPN · OFF`, `DO NOT DISTURB · OFF`,
    `NIGHT LIGHT · OFF`, `GAME MODE · OFF`.
  - sliders with labels: `OUTPUT · DT 880` (70%), `MIC · USB PNP` (55%), using `.sld`.
  - a now-playing row: lavender art square 44px, `Glassy Sky` / `SAPPHIRE`, prev/play/next icons.
  - footer row: `.micro` `CLOCKMOST · TANG OK · VAULT OK` left; right three 28px buttons: settings, lock, power.
- `clip`: the clipboard picker (Win+V) open under the clipboard icon: same panel look but its OWN recipe (rule:
  every component has its own design - not a copy of quick settings), 440px wide, ~600px tall:
  - head: `.label` `CLIPBOARD` + `.micro` `50 KEPT · IMAGES ON`, and a `.field.sm` search (`›››` prompt).
  - a list of 8 entries, numbered `01..08` in micro mono at the left (like the phonograph's track numbers):
    text snippets in body font, 1-2 lines, ellipsis; entry 02 is an IMAGE entry (a 120x68 thumbnail box with a
    simple drawn screenshot look + `PNG · 1920×1080` micro); entry 01 is selected = yellow block `.sel`; entry 05 is
    pinned (small pin glyph). Realistic contents: a git command, a URL `https://git.clockmost.com/...`, an IP
    `192.168.10.110`, a sentence from a chat, a hex colour `#FFFA00`.
  - footer keycaps: `.kbd` `↵` paste · `DEL` remove · `P` pin · `ESC` close, in micro.
- `cal`: clicking the clock: a small calendar panel centred under the clock (320px): month header `OCTOBER 2026`,
  7-column grid of days (mono), today (12) yellow block, weekend columns dimmer, below it 2 upcoming items in
  `.micro` (`TUE 13 · 21:00 FFXIV RAID`, `FRI 16 · BACKUP CHECK`).
