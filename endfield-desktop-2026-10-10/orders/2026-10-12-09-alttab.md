# Work order 09 - Alt+Tab switcher (layout mock)
Read `orders/STYLE.md` first (all of it). Build ONE file: `components/ds-alttab.html`. Slug for shots: `09`.
Mario liked Plasma's Alt+Tab because it shows WINDOW PREVIEWS. On `.stage` with 2-3 dimmed windows behind (simple
`.win.blur` frames), draw the switcher as held open:
- a full-screen dim layer (rgba(8,8,9,.55)).
- centred row of 5 window cards, each 300x200 preview area + caption below: the preview is a simplified miniature
  of that app (just blocks: Firefox = sidebar + content blocks; Discord = rail + list + bubbles; Phonograph =
  lavender square + list lines; Kitty = mono text lines; Dolphin = sidebar + rows). Caption: small app tile (letter
  or `firefox.svg`, 22px) + window title in `.label` style + `.micro` workspace `WS 02`.
- the SELECTED card (Discord, the 2nd card): yellow 2px frame around the preview + caption title in `--ink` + a
  small yellow block under the caption; others have a 1px faint frame and dimmer captions.
- the row sits on its own plate: a `.frost` bar behind the cards with 24px padding, corner brackets.
- under the row, centred: `.kbd` `ALT` + `.kbd` `TAB` next / `.kbd` `SHIFT` back / release to switch - in `.micro`.
- top of the plate: `.label` `WINDOWS · 5 OPEN · ALL WORKSPACES`.
